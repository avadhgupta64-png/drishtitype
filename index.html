How to use

First-time setup
  1. Sit about an arm’s length from the screen with your face well lit, then start tracking and allow the webcam.
  2. Calibration runs automatically: keep your head still and follow each dot with your eyes only.
  3. Move only your eyes — the pointer glides slowly to where you look. Head movement is ignored.
  4. Look at a letter or word. Blink twice to lock it for 2.6 seconds, then blink once more to select it. If you do not confirm in time, gaze control resumes.
  5. Suggested words work the same way. Press Speak message to read it aloud.
